# F24 verification

Open **Motion library → AI motion guide**, enter an idea, choose a style and duration, and copy the brief. The brief updates as you change the fields. Give the brief to the video tool or editor of your choice. Use the import workflow after export.

The reproducible sample is [Orbit Ignition](../024-first-mp4-work/quickstart.md). Its brief describes a six-second sequence. A fictional spacecraft in a cool-blue orbital hangar ignites and rises, then recedes into the starfield. The export uses 1920×1080 H.264, 30 fps and no audio.

With the packaged Studio and isolated launcher paths from the [F21 quickstart](../022-mp4-playback/quickstart.md), run Studio Checks with `--f24-guide-ui` and `--f24-sample-launch <delayed-target.exe>`. The first checks the two language modes and actual Windows clipboard. The second imports the same Orbit MP4, binds it to two isolated apps, previews that imported work and exercises both launch routes.
