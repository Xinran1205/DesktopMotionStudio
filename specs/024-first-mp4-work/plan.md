# F23 implementation plan

Author the sequence as an offline, deterministic Windows drawing program. Render RGB frames and encode H.264 with the existing fixture encoder. Use Studio's existing video metadata and library localization. The authoring tool is separate from the packaged player and takes an explicit encoder path.

## Creative direction

1. 0.0–1.7 s. A graphite hangar opens onto a starfield. Cyan runway lights lead toward a slender, symmetrical vehicle on the centerline.
2. 1.7–4.2 s. The engine ignites amber and cyan, then the vehicle rises past the concentric aperture. The camera follows its acceleration with the background moving in the opposite direction.
3. 4.2–6.0 s. The vehicle recedes into the orbital opening. Its trail compresses into a thin luminous point, with a clean silhouette remaining for the final fade.

Inspect key frames at 0.8, 2.7, 4.4 and 5.6 s. Test the authored file before committing the asset, then use the packaged Windows route and record the result.
